How do new engineers get up to speed on our auth system?
Your auth system uses OAuth 2.0 with PKCE flow for SPAs and client credentials for service-to-service. New engineers should start with the onboarding checklist in the Platform team's wiki, then review the auth service README for local setup.
auth-onboarding.md · platform-wiki
What's our retry policy for failed webhook deliveries?
Failed webhooks retry 5 times with exponential backoff: 1m, 5m, 30m, 2h, 24h. After 5 failures, the endpoint is marked inactive and an alert fires to #platform-alerts.
webhooks-runbook.md (line 89)
